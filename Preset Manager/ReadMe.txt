GUIDE TO USING THIS EXE

Everything:
Click the ''select file directory'' button and select the folder where the mod loads the presets.


True Random mode

Slide the sliders to your liking and just click randomize to generate a randomized preset!
Its name will be ''Random'' in your Among Us preset selector.
Keep in mind that you want to keep the actual random config file closed as it will sometimes not update correctly.


Random Premade Presets mode

Grab the presets you wish for the game to choose, and put them in the ''presets'' folder that comes along with this download.
Then, just click randomize to generate a random preset out of the ones in the folder! 
Its name will be ''Random'' in your Among Us preset selector.
